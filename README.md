# Backcountry Run

A 3D off-road driving game that runs in the browser. Built with Three.js.

Drive a quad, a pickup truck or a dirt bike around a valley with a river, forest trails and wildlife. The game modes are Racing (laps with medal times), Exploring, Rescue and Treasure hunt.

## Learning (gem houses)

Twelve gem houses hide around the valley. Stop at a door to get a question (read out loud), then a small counting or number job inside. Pick the subjects in Settings: Reading, Writing (spelling), Math, Science and Bible (a short character story first, then "which character is this?" later), plus Easier / Just right / Harder. A correct answer before the timer runs out earns 5 points; after the timer, 4.

- 🪙 Coins buy vehicles in the shop lot next to the garage (dune buggy, monster truck; aircraft coming later).
- 💎 Gems buy upgrades at the garage workbench (engine, tires, gas tank, paint).
- ⛽ Fuel fills the tank. When it runs dry you can still crawl along slowly.
- ⭐ Stars unlock superpowers: turbo, super jump, bear buddy, fuel saver.

Each player logs in with a name, password, age and grade, and the grade sets the starting difficulty. The "Who's playing?" list shows every time the game opens. Saves stay on the device and the web address they were made on, so always open the game from the same link (and not in private browsing). A parent can reset a forgotten password with the parent PIN.

Buying anything takes a second tap, and paint colors you already bought are free to switch back to. The Parent corner lists recent points earned and spent. The log cabin and red garage from the start also stand in the valley, with a 🏠 sign you can see from anywhere. Stop in front of the garage after a ride to hop off and walk around home. The 🔊 button at the top turns sound on and off; turning it off and on again always restarts the sound.

Settings → Parent corner is behind a 4-digit parent PIN. It sets daily limits (10 minutes riding and 30 minutes learning by default), a chore checklist where each tick adds 10 minutes of riding, the answer timer, read-aloud and free play, and shows what needs practice. When riding time runs out, practice questions continue until learning time is used up.

## Controls

- **Keyboard:** WASD or the arrow keys to drive, Space for the handbrake, R to reset, C to change the camera, H for the horn.
- **Touch:** the steering slider is on the left; the gas and brake slider is on the right.
- **Game controller:** RT or A for gas, LT or B to brake, left stick steers, RB handbrake, Y camera, X horn, LB reset, left stick click jump, right stick click turbo, View steps the arrow through the gems and home, Menu opens the menu, D-pad left/right highlights a vehicle (A switches to it at the start, B cancels), right stick looks around, left stick up/down also drives. At home: D-pad or LB/RB picks the rider, A chooses, X or Y rides the vehicle you're standing next to. In menus and windows the D-pad moves, A presses (and steps a dropdown to its next choice), B closes. Settings → Game controller shows a live button test.
